How It Works

From your first match to your next milestone.

Four steps, all live in the app today — no waitlist, no fake demo.

01

Create your profile

Sign up free, set your role — batter, bowler, all-rounder, keeper — and your area so nearby teams can find you.

02

Build or join a team

Search players by name or handle, send a request, and put together your squad — or get scouted for someone else's.

03

Score live, ball by ball

Run tournaments with real fixtures and net run rate, and share a live link so anyone can follow the match as it happens.

04

Climb the ranks

Every match builds your career stats and streak. From your local ground to the leaderboard — gully to gallery.
