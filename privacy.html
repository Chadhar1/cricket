Cricket Connect PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 August 2026

Cricket Connect is a cricket scoring app. This page explains, in plain terms, what data it holds, why, and what control you have over it.

The short version. If you use the app without an account, nothing leaves your device. If you create an account, your email, display name and match data are stored in Google Firebase so they sync across your devices. Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers, or used for tracking.

Using the app without an account

You can tap "Continue without an account" and use every scoring feature. In that mode all data — matches, teams, tournaments, fixtures — is stored only in your browser or app on that device. It is never transmitted anywhere. Clearing your browser data or uninstalling the app deletes it permanently, and it cannot be recovered.

What is collected if you create an account

  • Email address — to identify your account and let you sign in or reset a password.
  • Display name and profile picture — shown in the app. You may either pick one of twelve built-in illustrations, or upload a photo from your device. An uploaded photo is resized to 256 pixels, compressed, and stored with your profile. It is only visible to you until social features launch. You can remove it at any time from Profile.
  • Your cricket data — matches, scorecards, teams, player names you type, tournaments and scheduled fixtures.

If you sign in with Google, Google provides your email address and name to the app. Cricket Connect never receives your Google password.

What is not collected

  • No location data
  • No contacts, camera or microphone access. A profile photo is only read when you explicitly choose one from your gallery
  • No advertising identifiers, and no third-party advertising or analytics trackers
  • No payment information — the app is free and has no purchases

Who processes the data

Data is stored using Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), and the site is hosted on Vercel. Both act as service providers. Their handling is governed by Firebase's privacy terms and Vercel's privacy policy. Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.

Live match sharing

Live sharing is off by default and you switch it on per match. While it is on, that match's scorecard becomes readable by anyone holding the link, without signing in. This is the point of the feature, but it is worth being deliberate about: do not enable it for a match whose player names you would not want publicly visible.

Turning the toggle off removes the public copy immediately. Your private copy is unaffected. Only the match currently being shared is ever public — your account, other matches, teams and tournaments are never publicly readable.

Player names

You type in the names of people you play with. Please only enter names you are comfortable recording, and be mindful when scoring matches involving children. If someone asks you to remove their name, you can edit or delete the relevant team or match.

Your control over your data

  • Export — Profile → "Export all data (JSON)" downloads everything the app holds about you.
  • Delete individual items — teams, tournaments and scheduled matches can be deleted in the app.
  • Delete your whole account — email the address below and your account and all associated data will be deleted. There is no retention period; deletion is permanent.
  • Stop syncing — sign out. The app keeps working locally.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and accounts are not intended for them. Adults may of course record matches that children play in; see the note on player names above.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top will be updated. Continued use after a change means you accept the revised policy.
