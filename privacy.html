Cricket Connect PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 August 2026

Cricket Connect is a cricket scoring app. This page explains, in plain terms, what data it holds, why, and what control you have over it.

The short version. Browsing public tournaments, public player profiles and shared live matches doesn't require an account. Scoring a match, managing a team, and running a tournament does — creating an account stores your email, display name, general location and cricket data securely so it's available whenever you sign in. Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers, or used for tracking.

Browsing without an account

You don't need an account to look around: public tournament pages, public player profiles, and any live match a scorer has chosen to share are all viewable by anyone with the link, no sign-in required. Creating or managing your own matches, teams and tournaments, and using live scoring, requires a free account so your data can be kept securely against it and made available wherever you sign in.

What is collected if you create an account

  • Email address — to identify your account and let you sign in or reset a password.
  • Display name, username and profile picture — shown to other players once you set a username (needed to find friends or apply as an organiser). You may either pick one of twelve built-in illustrations, or upload a photo from your device. An uploaded photo is resized to 256 pixels, compressed, and stored with your profile. You can remove it, or change your username, at any time from Profile.
  • General location — country, province/state/emirate, district and tehsil/area, entered by you at signup or on your Profile. This is optional and is used to show relevant tournaments, grounds and promotions in your area — never your precise location, and never collected automatically from your device.
  • Your cricket data — matches, scorecards, teams, player names you type, tournaments and scheduled fixtures.
  • Friend connections and organiser applications — if you send or accept a friend request, or apply to run tournaments, that request and its status is stored against your account.
  • Feedback you submit — if you use the in-app Feedback form, your message, the category and rating you chose, and when you sent it are stored against your account so we can review and act on it. Feedback is used internally to improve the app and is never displayed publicly or shown to other users.

If you sign in with Google, Google provides your email address and name to the app. Cricket Connect never receives your Google password.

What is not collected

  • No precise or device location — no GPS, and no automatic location detection. The country/province/district/area fields above are optional, typed in by you, and only as specific as you choose to make them
  • No contacts, camera or microphone access. A profile photo is only read when you explicitly choose one from your gallery
  • No advertising identifiers, and no third-party advertising or analytics trackers
  • No payment information — the app is free and has no purchases

How your information is protected

Account information and your cricket data are stored securely and protected using industry-standard security measures, including encryption of data in transit (HTTPS) between your device and our servers. Access to your private account data — your matches, teams and tournaments — is restricted based on account permissions, so only you can normally read it. Administrators may access account or platform information where necessary for legitimate platform administration: organiser approval, content moderation, reviewing feedback, safety, support, or technical operation of the service — never to sell it or use it for advertising.

Private account data vs. your public player profile

These are deliberately kept separate. Your account — email address, sign-in details, private location fields, friend requests, organiser applications and feedback you've sent — is never publicly visible and is only ever readable by you and, where the section above applies, an administrator.

Once you choose a username, you additionally get a public player profile — your display name, username, avatar or profile photo, and your cricket statistics (matches, runs, wickets, teams and tournaments played). This is intentionally shareable: it's how other players find you, and how a profile link works when you share it. Your email address is never included on a public profile, and setting a username is entirely optional — scoring, teams and tournaments all work without one.

Live match sharing

Live sharing is off by default and you switch it on per match. While it is on, that match's scorecard becomes readable by anyone holding the link, without signing in. This is the point of the feature, but it is worth being deliberate about: do not enable it for a match whose player names you would not want publicly visible.

Turning the toggle off removes the public copy immediately. Your private copy is unaffected. Only the match currently being shared is ever public — your account, other matches, teams and tournaments are never publicly readable.

Player names

You type in the names of people you play with. Please only enter names you are comfortable recording, and be mindful when scoring matches involving children. If someone asks you to remove their name, you can edit or delete the relevant team or match.

Your control over your data

  • Delete individual items — teams, tournaments and scheduled matches can be deleted in the app.
  • Delete your whole account — email the address below and your account and all associated data will be deleted. There is no retention period; deletion is permanent.
  • Request a copy of your data — email the address below and we'll send you what the app holds about your account.
  • Stop syncing — sign out. Public content (tournaments, profiles, shared live matches) remains viewable as normal; your own account data stays exactly as it was until you sign back in.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and accounts are not intended for them. Adults may of course record matches that children play in; see the note on player names above.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top will be updated. Continued use after a change means you accept the revised policy.
