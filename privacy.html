Cricket Connect PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 October 2026

This policy explains what information Cricket Connect collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. Cricket Connect ("we", "us") is a cricket community app for players, teams and match organisers, available as an Android app and on the web. It is operated by Ghulam Abbas, based in the United Arab Emirates.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details — your name, email address, password (stored encrypted), and phone number if you choose to add one.
  • Profile details — profile photo, playing role (batter, bowler, all-rounder, wicketkeeper), batting and bowling style, city or area, and a short bio, if you choose to add them.
  • Cricket activity — teams you create or join, matches and tournaments you organise or play in, ball-by-ball scores, player statistics and results.
  • Messages and support requests you send to us.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and app information — device model, operating system version, app version, language, and crash or error reports.
  • Usage information — screens you visit, features you use, and daily reward activity, so we can run and improve the app.
  • Advertising ID — on Android, Google AdMob may collect your device's Advertising ID, IP address and general location (country or city level) to show ads. See section 4.

We do not collect your precise GPS location, contacts, photos (other than ones you choose to upload), or payment card details.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and sign you in.
  • To run core features: teams, match organising, live scoring, statistics and leaderboards.
  • To give daily rewards and track your progress.
  • To send you account emails, such as verification, password reset and important service updates.
  • To show ads that keep Cricket Connect free.
  • To fix bugs, prevent fraud and abuse, and keep the app secure.
  • To understand how the app is used so we can improve it.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. What other users can see

Cricket Connect is a community app. Your name, profile photo, playing role, teams, match scores and statistics can be seen by other users and, for public matches, by anyone viewing the scorecard. Your email address and phone number are never shown to other users.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Android app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google and its partners may use your device's Advertising ID and cookies to show ads, including personalised ads based on your interests, and to measure how ads perform.

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, we ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the app's settings.
  • On Android, you can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Learn more about how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Sports brands may also advertise or list products in the in-app shop. If you buy from a brand, you are dealing with that brand directly, and their own privacy policy applies to that purchase.

6. How long we keep your information

We keep your account information while your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your personal information within 30 days. Match scores you took part in may stay in other teams' match records, but your name is removed and shown as "Deleted player". Some information may be kept longer if the law requires it.

7. Your choices and rights

You can view and update most of your information in the app's profile settings. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to:

  • get a copy of your personal information;
  • correct information that is wrong;
  • delete your information;
  • object to or limit how we use it, including for personalised ads;
  • withdraw consent you have given.

To make a request, contact us using the details in section 12. We reply within 30 days.

8. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time:

  • In the app — go to Profile → Settings → Delete account.
  • By email — send a request from the email address on your account to the contact address below, with the subject "Delete my account".

This deletes your account, profile and personal information, as described in section 6.

9. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), encrypted password storage and access controls on our database to protect your information. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong password and keep it private.

10. Children

Cricket Connect is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. When we do, we update the effective date at the top. If the changes are significant, we will tell you in the app or by email.
